=== Requested changes ===

1. AI BUTTON STYLE NOW REPLACES THE STOCK BUTTON
   Root cause: the AI texture was painted as a background *inside* the stock gold
   button, so you saw art inside a gold frame with the logo on top of both.
   Fix: when a button style is applied the board gets .has-btnstyle, which removes the
   copper border, the inset box-shadow and the glass gloss entirely — the AI art IS
   the button — and the logo is inset to 17% so the generated bezel stays visible.
   The generation prompt also now asks for "ONE single empty square button with its own
   decorative bezel and edge... hollow flat centre, no logo, no text".
   Verified: border 0px, gloss display:none, box-shadow none.  [v4-button-style.png]

2. GRID STYLES (first section of Board Style)
   Buttons · Checker board · Checker no edge · Honeycomb · NFL logos only ·
   8 x 4 rectangles · Slanted rectangles. Each keeps whatever button style is applied.
   Verified on the overlay: 8x4 really renders 4 rows of 124x35 buttons; honeycomb
   offsets row 2 (via `left`, so it never fights the pop/slam transform); every grid
   keeps all 32 cells inside the 1080x165 band.
   [v4-grid-checker/honeycomb/logos/rect84/slant.png]

3. BUTTON SPACING
   Buttons touch by default (gap 0). Slider 0-20px; measured 0px at 0 and exactly 14px
   at 14.  [v4-gap-14.png]

4. HIGHLIGHTS
   Team Board has a "Tap mode: ELIMINATE / HIGHLIGHT" toggle plus "Clear highlights".
   With nothing highlighted, button animations play on all 32 buttons. Once any team is
   highlighted, they play ONLY on highlighted teams (ambient effects are suppressed on
   the rest; trigger effects are skipped for non-highlighted teams).
   Eliminating a team clears its highlight automatically — in the panel AND in deck.
   Deck actions added for the Stream Deck: highlight, unhighlight, highlight_toggle,
   highlight_clear (all accept &pc=N).  [v4-highlights.png]

5. VIDEO MODEL
   Root cause was two-fold. (a) LTX-Video is fal's cheapest/weakest model. (b) my brand
   prompt prefix was being prepended to effect clips, so the model rendered branded
   hero objects with invented gibberish text instead of the effect asked for.
   Fixes: Kling 1.6 standard (~$0.25) with a "Best — Kling 2 master" (~$1.40) option in
   the dropdown; effect prompts now say "isolated visual effect on pure solid black
   background, only the effect visible, no objects, no scene, no text" plus a negative
   prompt against text/watermarks/people.
   Also: sound and video now run through fal's QUEUE (submit + poll) so a 4-minute job
   can never hit the edge function's wall clock. The poll URLs come from fal's submit
   response — constructing them fails for nested model ids like kling-video/v1.6/....
   Verified end to end: a lightning-rim clip generated in 292s and came back as an
   isolated blue lightning rim on black with a hollow centre, exactly as prompted.

6. REFRAME EVERYWHERE + ZOOM
   Every image/video card (team styles, one-shots, banners, backgrounds, button styles,
   board backgrounds) has Reframe. The dialog gained a Zoom slider (100-300%) and shows
   the asset in the aspect ratio it will actually be displayed in.
   Verified: zoom 180% saved and the overlay rendered transform scale(1.8).
   [v4-reframe-zoom.png]
